# *[Pocket Watches]()* by [[Alan Shenton]]

<img src="http://books.google.com/books/content?id=ZRdAPAAACAAJ&printsec=frontcover&img=1&zoom=1&source=gbs_api" width=150>

`(New York: ACC Distribution, 1995), 0`

By 1893 those made for the railroads had to comply with the strictest of standards. (192)
- 18 or 16 size 
- minimum of 17 jewels 
- adjusted to 5 positions (sixth added later)
- keep time plus or minus 30 seconds per week 
- be adjusted to a temperature 40 to 95 degreed F
- have a double roller 
- be lever set 
- have winding stem at 12 o'clock 
- have a plain arabic dial with heavy hands
